Ini countdown shutdown yang memiliki opsi delay yang sudah ada atau custom delay

bisa dipasang di `taskschd.msc` kalau sering lupa mematikan pc/laptop

---

1. tekan `WIN + R` lalu ketik `taskschd.msc`

   <img width="399" height="206" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e11724f9-6e93-4578-9620-0da0a3ef5fc2" />

2. klik "Create Task"

   <img width="786" height="563" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e0c77867-dfd3-4f9d-bcd7-2f91b53d500f" />

3. pastikan "Run only when user is logged on" aktif, lalu masuk ke tab "Triggers"

   <img width="632" height="480" alt="Triggers" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5c9acd18-5fdb-41c0-a420-8aef66de6e64" />

4. Klik "New"

   <img width="632" height="480" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a8ab469-5102-4203-8d1c-de6ffbc930b8" />

5. Atur ke "Daily" atau sesuaikan sendiri. ubah jam sesuai yang diinginkan

   <img width="591" height="516" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a9c3c4f9-1d70-42e6-b23a-010b18bad596" />

   setelah itu klik "OK"

6. Masuk ke tab "Actions" lalu klik "New"

   <img width="632" height="480" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0ff230fd-4f81-4e11-9aed-c14c9614832c" />

7. "Action" set ke "Start a program", Program/script atur ke lokasi program

   <img width="454" height="500" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/02f1b62c-a075-4c7c-b8fc-244b05ff4fc1" />

   setelah itu klik "OK"

8. namakan task sesuai yang diinginkan

   <img width="632" height="480" alt="Penamaan" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/62ae6b9f-fd03-4af1-8903-d9aa4de20537" />

9. setelah semua selesai, klik "OK"

---

pastikan task ada.

bisa tes dengan klik "Run" cek apakah program bisa dijalankan
